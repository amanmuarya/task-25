More than just books. A community cornerstone.

Founded in 1982, Greenwood Public Library has evolved from a small neighborhood repository into a modern hub for learning, creation, and connection. Our philosophy centers on open access to information and fostering an environment where curiosity thrives unhindered.

The architectural redesign in 2020 opened up our spaces to natural light, breaking down traditional barriers. We believe the modern library is a dynamic ecosystem. It's a place where digital innovation meets classic literature, and where community dialog is just as important as silent reading.

Whether you're researching a thesis, bringing your toddler to their first storytelling session, or just looking for a quiet corner to escape the city noise, Greenwood welcomes you. Our doors remain a portal to infinite worlds.
